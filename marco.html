A note for you

Dear Marco,

I wanted to give you something that couldn't be found in any hotel gift shop — something with a real story and a real place behind it. This silk comes from Buriram, the province where I grew up in northeastern Thailand. The weavers there still raise silkworms by hand, ferment their own natural dyes from plants, and sit at wooden looms to create something that takes days to finish.

The elephant woven into the pattern holds deep meaning in Thai culture — it stands for memory, strength, and the wisdom to lead well. It felt right.

Thank you for this offsite and for the kind of leadership that travels well — across cultures, across countries, and across the distance between a global team and a local one.
